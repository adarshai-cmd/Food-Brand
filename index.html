FREQUENTLY POPPED QUESTIONS

Everything you wondered about whole fruits, happy tummies, and crisp bubbles.

100% whole organic fruit juice! We press cold-harvested Sicilian mandarins, Japanese yuzu, and Pacific marionberries. No "natural flavors" synthesized in test tubes, no chemical essences, no weird powdered shortcuts.

Prebiotics are plant-based soluble fibers (we use organic cassava root) that act as an all-you-can-eat gourmet buffet for your beneficial gut flora. They promote smooth digestion, immunity, and help keep blood sugar steady.

Only 30 to 38 calories per 12 oz can and 2g of naturally occurring fruit sugars. Zero cane sugar, zero high-fructose corn syrup, and zero fake metallic sweeteners like aspartame or concentrated stevia.

100% infinitely recyclable! Aluminum can be melted down and re-pressed into a brand new beverage can in as little as 60 days. No toxic plastic lining leaks, no landfill guilt.
